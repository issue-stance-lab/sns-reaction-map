# AI生成版の最終プロンプト

生成にはOpenAIの組み込み画像生成機能を使用した。

## A 編集地図

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: brand icon concept for a Japanese editorial website and social profile
Primary request: Create an original minimal brand mark representing one unresolved central question surrounded by four distinct viewpoints. Concept A: structured editorial map. A deep navy circular container holds one clearly open white ring at the center, four satellite nodes in blue, orange, green, and purple, and a few thick short connecting routes. The central ring must not read as a pie chart. The mark should feel trustworthy, analytical, calm, and memorable.
Style/medium: flat vector-friendly logo exploration, crisp geometric silhouette, editorial data-design character
Composition/framing: one centered circular badge, generous plain off-white margin, balanced at very small sizes
Color palette: deep navy #071A3D, blue #075EF2, orange #FF5426, green #36B8A3, purple #9358E8, white
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no people, no faces, no characters, no speech written inside, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup, no watermark, no extra symbols, original design only
Avoid: pie charts, globes, target symbols, compass arrows, thin fragile lines, stock app-icon appearance
```

## C 問いの羅針盤

```text
Use case: logo-brand
Asset type: brand icon concept for a Japanese editorial website and social profile
Primary request: Create an original minimal brand mark representing one unresolved central question surrounded by four distinct viewpoints. Concept C: question compass without arrows. Inside a deep navy circular container, place an open white central ring made from four separated curved segments, with four colored viewpoint nodes in blue, orange, green, and purple connected by short thick radial paths. Preserve visible negative-space gaps so the question remains open rather than resolved. The overall silhouette should be calm, intelligent, and distinctive.
Style/medium: flat vector-friendly logo exploration, bold modern editorial geometry, simple enough for a favicon
Composition/framing: one centered circular badge with generous plain off-white margin, symmetric base with a subtle dynamic rotation
Color palette: deep navy #071A3D, blue #075EF2, orange #FF5426, green #36B8A3, purple #9358E8, white
Constraints: no text, no letters, no numbers, no people, no faces, no characters, no pie chart, no gradients, no shadows, no mockup, no watermark, original design only
Avoid: compass arrows, targets, globes, location pins, flower shapes, thin fragile details, visual clutter, stock app-icon appearance
```
